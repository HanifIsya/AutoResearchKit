# Verified starter catalogue — purpose and scope

The 21 entries below are **general methodology and writing references**, NOT a topic-specific literature review. Checked for accessible landing pages and stated guidance on 2026-11-02. `Access` is intentionally narrower than "read every full text". Journal ranking must be checked separately per venue/year/category.

## Five high-priority exemplars

1. **Rougier et al. (2014), PLOS** — inspect caption quality and the idea that a figure must answer a question; [publisher article](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003833).
2. **Pautasso (2013), PLOS** — see thematic synthesis, reading notes, conceptual figure and review scope; [publisher article](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003149).
3. **IEEE Author Center** — understand difference between methods, results, tables and figures; [official guide](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-the-text-of-your-article/structure-your-article/).
4. **ACM CHI 2026** — caption must nearly stand alone; grayscale legibility and directly useful figures; [official guide](https://chi2026.acm.org/guide-to-a-successful-submission/).
5. **PRISMA 2020** — transparent record selection and flow-chart examples only for compatible review types; [guideline](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020).

## Selection pathways

| Use case | Priority source IDs | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| Essay/KTI | STD-01, STD-13, STD-14, STD-16 | every essay must use IMRaD or PRISMA |
| Empirical methods | STD-04, STD-07, STD-08, STD-20 | guidelines substitute for actual study design |
| Systematic review | STD-05, STD-06, STD-21 | a conceptual review has real PRISMA screening counts |
| Data science report | STD-09, STD-13, STD-15, STD-17 | artifact documentation means training was rerun |
| Citations and rank | STD-02, STD-03, STD-09, STD-10, STD-11, STD-12 | Scopus CiteScore/SJR quartile/SINTA identical |
| Ethics/authorship | STD-08, STD-18, STD-19 | AI tools can be listed as paper authors |
