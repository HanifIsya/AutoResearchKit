# Genre: Empirical journal article

Default candidate IMRaD if venue permits: title/abstract/keywords; Introduction; Methods; Results; Discussion; Conclusion (optional per journal); ethics, code/data access, disclosures, references and supplements. Choose appropriate design reporting framework, e.g. APA JARS for quantitative/qualitative/mixed; CONSORT randomized trial, STROBE observational health, TRIPOD+AI clinical prediction, PRISMA systematic reviews when applicable. Not one universal mandatory checklist.

The Methods section must allow reasoned appraisal of validity, with unit/eligibility/sampling/instrument/processing/analysis and relevant inferential assumptions; Results must report observed measured values/uncertainty; Discussion interprets and limits. Never paste the introduction of a source paper or cite a source on the basis of merely similar topic.

Select potential journal after comparing aims and scope, genre norms, article length, research design, readership, openness/fees, current indexing (with metric/category/year), ethical policies and figure specifications. No one can promise Q1/Q2 publication from language style alone.
